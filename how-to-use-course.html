Voice Biometric — Your First Step in Module 1

Before you start Module 1, you must register your voice biometric. Speak the fixed sentence that the system gives you into the microphone once. The system saves this voice biometric sample against your access code.

This voice sample becomes your identity for the whole course. The system uses it to confirm that you are the same student at your monthly test and at your final test. This step protects your certificate. It confirms that you, and only you, earned it.

Complete this step only once, at the very start. You do not repeat it later.

During your daily practice, other people can join you and help you in the exercises. At test time, the system evaluates only the voice that matches your registered voice biometric ID. It ignores any other voice.

How to Practice Effectively

  • Overcome Fear: Focus on your voice using the record and play features to practice self-correction without judgment.
  • Daily Routine: Dedicate consistent time every single day to step through your active speaking exercises.
  • Self-Critique: Treat your voice recordings like a mirror for your speech pattern adjustments.

Grammar Correction — For Every Module

Every module in this course carries its own grammar correction facility. Follow this exact process every day.

  • Write first. Prepare your everyday practice speech well. Write it out fully before you attempt it.
  • Practice outside, not from the page. Take your written speech and practice it outside, away from the screen. Do not read it out. Speak it into the microphone from memory.
  • Receive your correction. When you speak into the microphone, the system checks your speech and gives you grammar correction.
  • A paid service, free for our students. This grammar correction facility is a paid service in the real market. We provide it free for our students. Use it sparingly.
  • Six mistakes a day, no more. The system corrects up to six grammar mistakes per day. Use this limit sparingly. Do not waste your six corrections carelessly.
  • Study one module at a time. When you study the first module, use the exercises for that module only. Do not jump ahead. Do not mix in exercises from other modules.
  • Follow the course pattern, not your old habits. Follow the practice sentence model given in the course exactly. Do not use all your previous knowledge of English. Set aside what you learned before, and follow the pattern this course gives you.
  • Keep every sentence simple. Do not use passive voice. Do not use complex sentence constructions. Use a single verb in each sentence.
  • Cover every sentence type. Use questions, negatives, and statements regularly. Follow the pattern given in the modules for each of these three types.

Monthly Certification Test

On the first day of every month, a new test paper becomes available to you. The test paper stays open for 24 hours, GMT time, and closes after that. This test is oral only — you do not write your answers. The test paper appears in your selected language on the screen, but you must speak your answers out loud in English.

You can take the test three times within that same 24-hour period, every month. During these monthly attempts, do not submit your test. Use these five months only to practice and improve. Continue this pattern for five months. On the sixth month, check your preparation once, and then submit your test.

After you submit, the system generates your score and your certificate. Copy your certificate link and save it, or download it as a PDF. You can take this certification test again later if you need to, by paying 5000 USD. We set this fee high on purpose — we do not want to encourage students to retake this test.
