Course Instructions

Purpose of This Course

This is not a PDF. Not a textbook. Not an exam preparation module. Bilinglishsathya is for speaking. It is for becoming fluent in English in every real life situation — at work, at home, with friends, while travelling, anywhere. You speak. You hear yourself. You improve.

How to Practice Effectively

  • Overcome Fear: Focus on your voice using the record and play features to practice self-correction without judgment.
  • Daily Routine: Dedicate consistent time every single day to step through your active speaking exercises.
  • Self-Critique: Treat your voice recordings like a mirror for your speech pattern adjustments.
