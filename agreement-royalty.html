A non-refundable advance, calculated as royalty on 100 student enrollments or ₹25,00,000 (whichever is higher), secures exclusive territorial marketing rights and is credited against ongoing per-transaction royalty as actual sales occur. This protects the Trust's position fully from day one, while giving the Territory Partner a clear, proportional path to growing margin as their own sales increase.

2.1 Advance Royalty — Mechanics

To secure exclusive territorial marketing rights, a Territory Partner is liable for royalty on a minimum of 100 student enrollments, or ₹25,00,000 (or the equivalent in the relevant international currency), whichever is higher, payable upon signing. This advance is non-refundable under any circumstances, consistent with standard practice for securing exclusive access to a valuable, limited asset. At the fixed 25% royalty rate on the current domestic price, royalty on 100 students equals ₹25,00,000 — the two figures align at current pricing; if course pricing changes, the higher of the two amounts always applies. This is not a separate fee in addition to royalty — it is royalty, paid as a guaranteed minimum up front, credited against actual per-transaction royalty as real sales occur.

At the end of the first 12-month period, the advance is reconciled against actual royalty accrued from real sales during the year. If actual royalty exceeds ₹25,00,000, the Territory Partner pays the excess in the normal per-transaction manner — the advance is not a cap. If actual royalty is less than ₹25,00,000, no refund or credit is due to the Territory Partner; the advance is retained in full, as the price of securing exclusive territorial access for the year, whether or not that access was fully utilized. This reconciliation determines only what, if anything, is owed beyond the advance — it never results in money being returned to the Territory Partner.

2.2 Payment Gateway & Access Control

  • All payments from end users for access to the Course, in any territory, shall be processed exclusively through payment gateways owned and controlled by the Trust (currently Razorpay and PayPal). The Licensee shall never operate, control, or replicate an independent payment collection or access-granting system for the Course.
  • Access to the Course shall be granted only through the Trust's own access-control system. The Trust retains sole control of the access-code generation logic and any associated cryptographic secrets at all times.
  • Royalty is settled as and when each student payment is received, not accumulated and paid in batches. Because all payments route through the Trust's designated gateway, the Trust's 25% royalty share is realized directly at the point of each transaction, with the remaining balance due to the Territory Partner. No separate audit process is required, as the payment gateway itself provides verified sales data at the time of each transaction, and the Trust's own gateway records shall prevail over any Licensee-reported figures in the event of any discrepancy.

2.3 Nature of Territorial Rights (Digital Product Clarification)

Because the Course is delivered digitally, geographic territory cannot function as a physical access restriction the way it might for a tangible product. A Territory granted under this Agreement refers to the Licensee's exclusive right to market, advertise, and solicit sales within that geography — it does not, and cannot, prevent a student located anywhere in the world from accessing the Trust's website or purchasing directly through the Trust's own channels. Sales made directly through the Trust's own website or gateways, without referral from or active solicitation by a Territory Partner, remain the Trust's own sales and are not owed to any Territory Partner. A sale is attributed to a Territory Partner's royalty calculation only where reasonably traceable to that partner's specific marketing activity, referral link, or documented lead; where attribution is unclear, the sale is treated as a direct Trust sale. The Trust does not guarantee that a Territory Partner will be the sole source of course access for that geography, given the inherently borderless nature of digital distribution — the value of the license lies in exclusive marketing rights and the locally-tailored sales relationship the partner builds.

2.3.1 Universal Payment-Gated Access

Access to the Course is available to any student, anywhere in the world, on payment of the applicable course fee through the Trust's or a Territory Partner's designated payment gateway, regardless of the student's nationality, location, or country of residence, and regardless of whether that country is presently covered by an active Territory Partner. No Territory Partner may restrict, block, or condition a student's access to the Course on any basis other than payment of the applicable fee. Where a student's country has no active Territory Partner, the student may access the Course directly through the Trust's own channels, on the same payment-gated basis. This ensures the Course remains available, without geographic barrier, to any student who has paid for it — including a student who relocates from one country to another for study, work, or any other purpose, whose access, once purchased, is not tied to or restricted by their location at the time of purchase or at any point thereafter.

2.3.2 Cross-Territory Access Disputes

Because access under Clause 2.3.1 is deliberately unrestricted by geography, questions may arise as to which Territory Partner, if any, a given student's enrollment should be attributed to for royalty purposes — for example, where a student purchases while physically present in one Territory Partner's region but is a resident, citizen, or long-term marketing target of another. Any such cross-access dispute between Territory Partners, or between a Territory Partner and the Trust, regarding attribution of a specific enrollment shall be resolved at the sole discretion of the Chief Trustee, whose determination shall be final and binding on all Territory Partners. The Chief Trustee shall exercise this discretion in the interest of the student's uninterrupted access to the Course, and shall not be influenced by which outcome yields a larger Royalty to the Trust, since the Trust's own interest in any such dispute is limited to the fixed 25% Royalty regardless of which Territory Partner the enrollment is attributed to. This discretion applies specifically to cross-territory attribution questions arising under Clause 2.3.1, and is distinct from, and does not limit, the general dispute resolution process set out in Section 12 for other matters.

2.4 No Expansion Without Consent

The Licensee's rights are limited to marketing, promotion, and facilitating sales of the Course as it currently exists within the licensed territory. The Licensee shall not create, commission, or authorize any new course, expanded curriculum, or material modification to the Course without the Chief Trustee's prior written consent.

The Trust has no objection in principle, provided any such arrangement is proposed to and approved in writing by the Trust before it takes effect; the original Territory Partner remains responsible for ensuring the sub-licensed party adheres to the same terms of this Agreement; and the fixed 25% royalty applies to all territory sales regardless of how many parties are involved. The Trust reserves the right to decline any proposed sub-license or delegation at its discretion.

If a territory reverts to the Trust at the end of a term because the original Licensee does not renew, or the Trust elects not to renew with that Licensee, the Trust is free to grant a fresh license for that same territory to a different, new party, on terms negotiated afresh and not bound by, or owed to, the original Licensee in any way.

2.7 Year 1 Market Entry & Future Scaling — The Growth Ladder

The Trust's licensing terms are designed to scale up gradually and predictably as a territory proves itself, rather than jumping to a materially higher commitment at once. This gives a Territory Partner a clear, foreseeable path as their market grows, and gives the Trust growing returns that track real, demonstrated demand.

Year 1Advance Royalty of ₹25,00,000 (or royalty on 100 students, whichever is higher), as described in Clause 2.1. Royalty fixed at 25% throughout.
Renewal — Year 2If Year 1 sales met or exceeded 100 students, the Year 2 advance is set at the greater of the Year 1 advance or royalty on the Territory Partner's own actual Year 1 enrollment figure — i.e. the floor rises only as far as the territory has already proven itself, not beyond it. If Year 1 sales fell short of 100 students, the Trust and Territory Partner shall discuss the Year 2 advance in good faith, which may remain unchanged, taking into account the reasons for the shortfall and the partner's plans for the coming year.
Renewal — Year 3 onwardThe same step logic continues at each annual renewal: the advance is recalibrated to the greater of the prior year's advance or royalty on the most recently completed year's actual enrollment, so that increases always track real, demonstrated growth in that specific territory rather than a fixed or arbitrary escalation.
Established, high-volume territoriesOnce a territory has sustained strong enrollment over multiple years, the Trust may, at its discretion, discuss materially larger commitments with the Territory Partner — including extended terms, broader segment rights, or equity-linked arrangements as described below — but only as a further step up the same ladder, never as a precondition to entry.

The 25% royalty rate itself never changes at any step of this ladder. Only the advance and MGR floor move, and only in the direction and to the extent that actual sales in that territory have already justified. If a territory attracts interest from multiple prospective partners once viability is proven, the Trust may open the territory to competitive terms before selecting or renewing a partner. In territories where sustained volume and competition justify it, the Trust may also consider equity-linked or profit-share arrangements with a Territory Partner, in place of or in addition to standard licensing terms, entirely at the Trust's discretion.

2.8 Market Opportunity — Mandatory Education, Missing Fluency

In several of the world's largest economies — including Japan, South Korea, China, and Germany — English is a compulsory subject taught for a decade or more within the national school curriculum. Despite this, formal instruction in these systems is typically grammar-heavy, exam-focused, and delivered through memorization rather than spoken practice, leaving the great majority of students without genuine conversational fluency even after ten to fifteen years of study. BILINGLISHSATHYA does not compete with these mandatory curricula; it fills the specific gap they consistently fail to close — real spoken fluency. This makes such territories especially well-suited candidates for territorial licensing, as the underlying demand for spoken English has already been created and sustained by each country's own education system.

Territory, advance amount, and any deal-specific terms shall be confirmed by both Parties initialling the Confirmation Block at Clause 15.

Section 2.5

Grammar-Check Feature — Usage Limits & Platform Maintenance Costs

2.5.1 Usage Allowance

  • Each enrolled learner's access to the Grammar-Check Feature begins on the date of that learner's first use, and remains available for fifty (50) consecutive calendar days from that date only.
  • Within this 50-day window, each learner is entitled to a maximum of twenty (20) grammar corrections per calendar day. This daily allowance does not carry over or accumulate if unused.
  • After the 50-day window has elapsed, the Grammar-Check Feature is disabled for that learner permanently. All other course content, including all fifty day-lessons, recording and playback, and the accent-score display, remains available for the full six (6) month course access period regardless of Grammar-Check Feature expiry.
  • A learner wishing to continue using the Course beyond their initial six-month access period may renew or extend access on payment of the applicable renewal fee, as published on the Trust's website and course platform from time to time. Such renewals constitute a further sale under this Agreement, and Royalty is payable on them in the same manner, and at the same fixed rate, as on any initial enrollment. The Territory Partner is licensed to market and facilitate such renewals within their territory on the same basis as initial course sales.

2.5.2 Platform Maintenance Costs — Borne Separately by the Licensee

  • The ongoing platform maintenance costs described in the table below (payment processing, website hosting, source code repository, and the Grammar-Check AI service) are separate and distinct from, and shall not reduce, dilute, or be deducted from, the Royalty payable to the Trust under Section 7. The Royalty is calculated on full Gross Revenue with no deductions whatsoever, exactly as defined in Section 3 and Clause 7.1.
  • The Licensee shall bear such platform maintenance costs as are attributable to their own licensed territory, separate from and in addition to the Advance Royalty and ongoing Royalty described in Section 2.
  • The Trust shall invoice the Licensee for actual platform maintenance costs incurred on the Licensee's behalf on a quarterly basis, payable within 30 days of invoice, alongside — but never combined or netted against — the Licensee's per-transaction Royalty obligations.

Where a retest is availed, it constitutes a further sale under this Agreement, and Royalty is payable on it in the same manner, and at the same fixed rate, as on any initial enrollment. Section 7

Financial Terms

7.1 Royalty Settlement — Per Transaction

  • Royalty is settled as and when each student payment is received, not accumulated and paid in batches. Because all student payments route through the Trust's designated gateway (PayPal for international, Razorpay for domestic), the Trust's 25% royalty share is realized directly at the point of each transaction, with the remaining balance due to the Territory Partner.
  • For example, at the current domestic course price of ₹1,00,000, a 25% royalty equals ₹25,000 per student enrollment. For international sales, the 25% royalty is calculated proportionally on the applicable transaction amount. If course pricing changes for any territory in future, the royalty amount adjusts automatically, as it is always calculated as 25% of actual transaction value.
  • Royalties shall never be reduced by the Licensee's costs, discounts, taxes, or payment gateway fees.

7.2 Advance Royalty and Minimum Guaranteed Royalty

The advance royalty described in Clause 2.1 is payable upon signing and is credited against per-transaction royalty as actual sales occur, subject to the non-refundable terms set out there. In addition, each Territory Partner agrees to a Minimum Guaranteed Royalty (MGR) — a minimum annual royalty floor set per territory. If actual royalty realized from sales in a given year falls below this floor, the Territory Partner pays the difference to meet the MGR. The MGR exists to protect against inactive or dormant licenses and does not replace or delay per-transaction settlement, which applies from the first sale onward.

7.3 Payment Verification

Because all student payments are routed directly through the Trust's designated PayPal (international) or Razorpay (domestic) gateway, the Trust receives direct, independent confirmation of every sale, and royalty calculations are based on this record. No separate audit process is required, as the payment gateway itself provides verified sales data at the time of each transaction.

7.4 Fee Enhancement

The Chief Trustee may revise end-user pricing upward at any time in response to market demand, inflation, or enhanced course value. The Licensee shall implement revised pricing within 30 days of written notice.

7.5 Operational Infrastructure — Borne by the Territory Partner

The Course is delivered through infrastructure owned and controlled solely by the Trust, comprising the GitHub source code repository, Vercel website hosting, Razorpay and PayPal payment gateways, and the Grammar-Check AI service. As set out in Clause 2.5.2 and the accompanying cost table, the ongoing costs of this infrastructure attributable to the Territory Partner's own licensed territory — including payment processing charges, hosting costs beyond free-tier thresholds, repository costs where apportioned, and Grammar-Check AI usage costs within the limits described in Clause 2.5.1 — are borne by the Territory Partner, separately from and in addition to the Advance Royalty and ongoing Royalty. These costs are invoiced by the Trust quarterly, as described in Clause 2.5.2, and are never netted or adjusted against Royalty due to the Trust.
