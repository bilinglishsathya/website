Overview

A Platform, Not Just a Course

BILINGLISHSATHYA was built as a spoken English course. But the infrastructure, methodology, and pedagogical framework that powers it is far larger than one subject. This document outlines the strategic extensions available to licensees who wish to grow beyond the initial offering — and the additional licensing opportunities these extensions represent.

Breakthrough Innovation

The Reverse Methodology — Any Language Through English

The BILINGLISHSATHYA methodology was built to teach English using the learner's mother tongue as a bridge. The same methodology can be reversed — using English as the base to teach any world language to English-speaking learners.

This is not a modification. It is a complete second product line — built on the same proven 50-day structure, the same assessment system, and the same certificate platform. No new infrastructure required.

Areas of Extension

New Course Verticals

The following course extensions can be developed and licensed independently or as bundled packages, each carrying its own pricing and royalty structure.

01

Business English

High Value Corporate

A structured course for professionals — meetings, emails, presentations, negotiations. Corporate buyers pay USD 500 to USD 5,000 per participant for business language training. This vertical targets companies directly.

  • 50-day format retained
  • Workplace scenarios as teaching context
  • Certificate for corporate HR records
  • Bulk licensing for employee batches
02

Interview English

Career & Employment

Focused on job seekers — answering questions confidently, structuring responses, tone and body language awareness. Massive demand in India and South East Asia where English interviews are the gateway to employment.

  • Mock interview question bank
  • Industry-specific modules
  • Spoken test simulates real interview
03

IELTS / TOEFL Preparation

International Examination

The spoken English foundation BILINGLISHSATHYA provides is the ideal preparation base for IELTS Speaking and TOEFL iBT. An examination-focused extension leverages the existing methodology with test-specific practice.

04

Mother Tongue Preservation Courses

NRI Diaspora

NRI parents worldwide want their children to speak their mother tongue — Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam. Using English as the teaching base, BILINGLISHSATHYA can deliver structured mother tongue courses to diaspora communities globally.

  • Emotional connection — identity and culture
  • Parents will pay premium for this
  • Untapped global market
05

Personality Development & Public Speaking

Soft Skills

Dr. Thottempadi's 18+ years of teaching experience extends naturally into communication, confidence, and public speaking. A course that complements the spoken English foundation with broader life skills.

06

Continuous Assessment Packages

Recurring Revenue

The existing spoken test and certificate system can be offered as a standalone product — independent of the course. Institutions, companies, and individuals can purchase test packages for ongoing assessment and certification.

  • Single test purchase
  • 3-test or 5-test packages
  • Annual unlimited assessment subscriptions
  • Institutional bulk test licensing
  • This creates perpetual revenue beyond the one-time course sale

Licensing Framework

Terms for Extension Licensing

Extension courses and new verticals are available for licensing separately from the core BILINGLISHSATHYA spoken English course. The following terms apply to all extension agreements.

IP Ownership All course content, methodology, structure, and brand remain the sole property of Dr. Sathyalakshmi Thottempadi in perpetuity.
Royalty — All Extensions 25% of all revenue generated from extension courses, automatically scaled to any price change by the licensee. No renegotiation required.
Modification Rights Dr. Thottempadi retains the right to modify, update, improve, or expand any course content at any time. Licensee benefits from all updates automatically.
License Term 10 years per territory per course vertical. Rights revert fully to the IP owner upon expiry.
Exclusivity One licensee per territory per course vertical. No competing licenses issued for the same vertical in the same territory.
Sublicensing Permitted with prior written approval from Dr. Thottempadi. Royalty applies to all sublicensed revenue.
New Verticals Development of new course verticals remains exclusively at the discretion of Dr. Thottempadi. Licensees may express interest; development is not guaranteed.
Assessment System The spoken test and certificate system may be licensed independently as a standalone assessment product, subject to separate negotiation.
