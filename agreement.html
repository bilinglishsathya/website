2.1 Payment Gateway & Access Control

  • All payments from end users for access to the Course, in any territory, shall be processed exclusively through payment gateways owned and controlled by the Trust (currently Razorpay and PayPal). The Licensee shall never operate, control, or replicate an independent payment collection or access-granting system for the Course.
  • Access to the Course shall be granted only through the Trust's own access-control system. The Trust retains sole control of the access-code generation logic and any associated cryptographic secrets at all times.
  • Each territory licensee shall be assigned a unique tracked referral link. Sales attributed to that link, as recorded in the Trust's own payment gateway and access-code records, shall be the sole basis for calculating royalties and territory performance. The Trust's own records shall prevail over any Licensee-reported figures in the event of any discrepancy.

2.2 No Expansion Without Consent

The Licensee's rights under this Option are limited to marketing, promotion, and facilitating sales of the Course as it currently exists within the licensed territory. The Licensee shall not create, commission, or authorize any new course, expanded curriculum, or material modification to the Course without the Chief Trustee's prior written consent.

2.3 Re-Licensing to a New Party

If a territory reverts to the Trust at the end of a term because the original Licensee does not renew, or the Trust elects not to renew with that Licensee, the Trust is free to grant a fresh Option B license for that same territory to a different, new party. The fee for such a new license shall be negotiated afresh with the new party, based on the demonstrated market demand and results established during the prior Licensee's term, and is not bound by, or owed to, the original Licensee in any way. The 20% Royalty under Clause 2.1 applies to the new party exactly as it applied to the original Licensee.

Option B may be entered into for multiple territories simultaneously, each governed by its own Schedule specifying territory, fee, and effective dates, without affecting the Trust's rights under Option A below.

2.4 Market Opportunity — Mandatory Education, Missing Fluency

In several of the world's largest economies — including Japan, South Korea, China, and Germany — English is a compulsory subject taught for a decade or more within the national school curriculum. Despite this, formal instruction in these systems is typically grammar-heavy, exam-focused, and delivered through memorization rather than spoken practice, leaving the great majority of students without genuine conversational fluency even after ten to fifteen years of study. BILINGLISHSATHYA does not compete with these mandatory curricula; it fills the specific gap they consistently fail to close — real spoken fluency. This makes such territories especially well-suited candidates for Option B licensing, as the underlying demand for spoken English has already been created and sustained by each country's own education system.

2.4 Option A — Worldwide Rights (Perpetual)

The Trust's ultimate ambition remains Option A — Worldwide Rights in perpetuity, covering literally all countries across the globe without exception, for the full term of copyright protection. Option B above is not a discount of this ambition, but a deliberate strategy to make BILINGLISHSATHYA accessible to smaller entrepreneurs and regional partners, allowing the Trust's global reach to be built incrementally — one territory and one term at a time — while the Trust's long-term value is protected and grown through the Royalty regardless of the scale of any individual agreement.

Reversion of Rights

Section 7

Financial Terms

7.1 Royalty Payments

  • Royalties shall be paid quarterly within 30 days after each calendar quarter end.
  • Each payment shall be accompanied by a Chartered Accountant verified Statement of Sales.
  • Late payments shall attract interest at 18% per annum from the due date.
  • Royalties shall never be reduced by the Licensee's costs, discounts, taxes, or payment gateway fees.

7.2 Fee Enhancement

The Chief Trustee may revise end-user pricing upward at any time in response to market demand, inflation, or enhanced course value. The Licensee shall implement revised pricing within 30 days of written notice.

7.3 Audit Rights

The Trust may appoint an independent auditor with 15 days' notice to inspect the Licensee's financial records relating to Course sales. If underpayment exceeds 5%, the Licensee bears all audit costs plus the shortfall with interest at 18% per annum.
