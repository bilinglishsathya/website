Section 2

Territory Licensing & Royalty Structure

The Trust grants exclusive territorial marketing rights on a royalty-first model. Securing a territory requires a non-refundable advance equivalent to royalty on 100 enrollments or ₹25,00,000 (whichever is higher), credited against ongoing per-transaction royalties.

Commercial Terms
Royalty-First Territory License
Advance RoyaltyRoyalty on 100 students or ₹25,00,000 (non-refundable)
Ongoing RoyaltyFixed 25% of Gross Revenue across all transactions
Payment RoutingMandatory via Trust Gateways (Razorpay / PayPal)

2.1 Digital Access & Scope

Because the course is digital, territory grants cover active marketing within the region. Direct unassigned sales via Trust channels remain with the Trust. Detailed growth frameworks and regional market potentials are outlined in our Scope of Development.

Section 2A

Grammar-Check & Certification Standards

  • Grammar-Check Feature: Active for 50 consecutive calendar days from first learner use, capped at 20 corrections per day. Platform and AI maintenance costs are invoiced quarterly to the Licensee separately from Royalty.
  • Certification Test: Digital, spoken monthly assessments. First 6 months include free attempts. Subsequent retests incur a high-value protection fee structured as a standard revenue-share sale. Full guidelines are detailed on the Test Procedure page.

Section 5

Intellectual Property & Trade Secret

The Trust retains absolute ownership of all trademarks, software, and content. Licensees agree never to reverse-engineer or disclose the proprietary drill methodology.

Section 6

Reversion of Rights

Upon agreement expiration or termination, all distribution rights revert automatically to the Trust under applicable copyright provisions.

Section 7

Financial Terms & Taxes

  • Settlement: Realized per-transaction via gateway; 25% is retained/remitted immediately to the Trust.
  • Taxes: Each party independently handles local tax compliance (GST, VAT, Corporate Income Tax) without deduction from amounts owed to the Trust.

Section 8

Section 9
